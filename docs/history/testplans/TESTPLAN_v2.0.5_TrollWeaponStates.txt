1. Start Valheim and confirm log says Loading [Valheim_Workers_Mount 2.0.5].
2. Enter Troll symbiosis 5-10 times. Every entry must be visually unarmed regardless of vanilla random Troll variant.
3. Fell a tree and look at the fallen TreeLog. [E] Baumstamm aufnehmen must appear.
4. Press E. The Troll must switch to a visible log-club state; log should contain Troll2/native-log state equipped when native weapon exists, otherwise visibleWeapon=True via mesh fallback.
5. Use 12 attacks. The log breaks and Troll returns to unarmed.
6. Pick up another log and leave symbiosis before it breaks. The held log is dropped back into the world.
