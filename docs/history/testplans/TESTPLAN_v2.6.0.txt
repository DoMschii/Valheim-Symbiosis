v2.6.0 locomotion test

SERPENT
1. Enter Serpent; hold W and aim up/down: body must turn and swim along look direction.
2. Hold S: reverse must be possible but very slow (~16% forward speed).
3. Turn camera 180 degrees then W: Serpent should turn and travel quickly in the new direction.
4. Verify real depth hold still works after releasing controls.

DRAKE
1. W without Shift: cruise should be clearly faster than v2.5.1.
2. Shift+W: Drake must pitch downward and accelerate into a fast dive.
3. Shift+W+Space: dive remains downward; Space must not turn it into an upward sprint.
4. Release Shift: normal aim-led flight resumes.
