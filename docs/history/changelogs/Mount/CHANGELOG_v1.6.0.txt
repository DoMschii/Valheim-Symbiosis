Valheim_Workers_Mount v1.6.0 - Unified movement stamina

- Drake Shift flight boost now costs 10 player stamina per second while moving.
- Sea Serpent Shift swim boost now costs 8 player stamina per second while swimming directionally.
- Exhausted boost falls back to normal movement; it never stops or locks the form.
- Base movement and vertical controls remain free.
- Troll (16/s) and Werewolf (12/s) sprint stamina stay unchanged, so all four forms now use one consistent movement-resource model.
- No new scene scans, Physics polling or per-frame allocations.
- v1.5.0 cooldown integrity and all prior switching/interaction/consumable safeguards remain preserved.
