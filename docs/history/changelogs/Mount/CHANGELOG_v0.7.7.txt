v0.7.7 - Vanilla Third Person controls for Werewolf + Sea Serpent

- Third Person W/A/S/D is camera-relative like the normal Valheim player.
- Werewolf and Sea Serpent body/look yaw follows actual movement direction.
- S turns the creature around and moves forward in that direction; A/D turn left/right instead of strafing.
- Free Third Person camera no longer forces creature facing.
- Ego keeps the existing reverse/strafe behaviour.
- Drake/Troll behaviour is unchanged.
