Valheim Workers Mount v0.1.6

- Reworked Drake steering to an aim-to-fly model.
- Mouse yaw/pitch now defines the full 3D flight direction in both ego and third person.
- W flies forward along the view axis; S flies backward along it.
- A/D no longer rotate or strafe the Drake, removing the fixed-axis steering conflict.
- Looking up/down while holding W now climbs/dives naturally.
- Third-person camera orbits using mouse aim rather than trailing a separate body-yaw axis.
- Drake body smoothly turns toward the same mouse-defined flight direction.
- Space/Ctrl/C remain optional direct altitude correction controls.
