Valheim_Workers_Mount v1.1.0 - Unified form combat HUD

- Adds the compact 3-slot cooldown HUD to every symbiosis form, not only Werewolf.
- Drake: LMB Frost, RMB Eisbolzen, MMB Eisfaecher.
- Troll: LMB Schlag, RMB Bodenschlag, MMB Gebruell.
- Sea Serpent: LMB Biss, RMB Bissstoss, MMB Schwanzfeger.
- Werewolf keeps Klaue, Sprung-Angriff and Blutjagd with active combo progress.
- HUD reads existing runtime cooldown timers only; no world scans, Physics queries or additional AI polling.
- All v1.0.0 controls, combat, camera, darkness, consumables and build-mode fixes are preserved.
