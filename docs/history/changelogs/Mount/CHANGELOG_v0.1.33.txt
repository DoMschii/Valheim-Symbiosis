Valheim_Workers_Mount v0.1.33

- Added player-controlled Drake combat during symbiosis.
- Left mouse button fires the vanilla-style Drake frost salvo: three hatchling_cold_projectile shots along the camera aim direction.
- Frost salvo uses 15 m/s projectile speed, 90 frost damage per projectile and a 6 second cooldown.
- The centre projectile follows exact aim; side projectiles use mirrored spread for predictable player control.
- Attacks are disabled while menus or the transformation sequence are active.
- Right mouse button remains free for a future secondary/special Drake ability.
