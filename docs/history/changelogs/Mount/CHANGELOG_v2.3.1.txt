v2.3.1 - Per-form Ego fine-tune reset controls

- SmartCore F8 > Ego now shows the saved XYZ Ego fine-tune values for Drake, Troll, Werewolf and Sea Serpent.
- Each creature has its own "Zuruecksetzen" button.
- Reset only clears that creature's persisted XYZ fine-tune to (0,0,0); the other forms are untouched.
- Changes apply immediately and are saved to BepInEx config.
- Drake and Troll positions remain exactly as calibrated unless their own reset button is pressed.
- All v2.3.0 Serpent pitch-swim behavior and v2.2.5 accelerated live tuning are preserved.
