Valheim_Workers_Mount v2.7.0 - Drake Flight Feel

- Drake normal W flight follows camera pitch inside a natural flight cone instead of allowing accidental near-vertical movement.
- Shift+W remains the dedicated Sturzflug and now commits to a slightly steeper minimum dive angle.

- Control hint now says Shift+W Sturzflug instead of generic sprint.
- Head pose/camera anchor from v2.6.6-v2.6.7, wingbeat propulsion, attacks and all other creature forms are unchanged.
