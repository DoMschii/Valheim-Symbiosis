Valheim_Workers_Mount v0.1.27

- Added a smooth symbiosis transformation instead of an instant body/camera swap.
- Entering symbiosis now eases Camera.main from the current human view into the Drake eye position over ~1.05 s.
- The human body remains visible during the first half of the transition, then hands off to the Drake.
- Remote temporary Drake avatars grow smoothly from a small visual scale to full size during the hand-off.
- Flight input/F6/E are locked until the transformation completes, preventing movement during the camera/body switch.
- Returning from symbiosis runs the transition in reverse: camera eases back to the remembered human camera rig, the human reappears, and a temporary Drake shrinks before it is removed.
- Emergency exits (portal travel, lost ownership, shutdown) still bypass the animation for safety.
- No Workers core changes required; remains compatible with core v0.5.118.
