v2.0.7 - Deterministic Troll1/Troll2 body swap

- Removes GiveDefaultItem/native item materialization from the active Troll pickup path.
- Builds two runtime Troll prefab templates from vanilla Troll.m_randomSets:
  Troll1 keeps only non-log attack sets; Troll2 keeps only the native troll_log_swing set.
- Troll symbiosis always starts from Troll1.
- E on a fallen log swaps the active temporary body Troll1 -> Troll2 at the exact same transform while keeping bound level, rider/player authority and camera.
- The world log is consumed only after the Troll2 body was created successfully.
- After 12 swings the body swaps Troll2 -> Troll1.
- Existing stamina, star scaling, cooldowns, Serpent stamina fix and lifecycle safety remain preserved.
