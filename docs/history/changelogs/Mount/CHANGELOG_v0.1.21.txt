Valheim_Workers_Mount v0.1.21
- Personal Drake selection is now persisted locally per Valheim player ID, so the whistle still knows that a Mount exists after portals, long-distance travel and zone unloads.
- The whistle remains only the Symbiosis hotkey; it does not recall or teleport the physical Worker.
- If the assigned physical Drake is loaded, Symbiosis uses it as before.
- If the assigned Drake is not loaded, Symbiosis creates a temporary Drake avatar at the player for flight while the real Worker remains at its original world location.
- Temporary Symbiosis avatars are destroyed on dismount/shutdown.
- Pressing the whistle hotbar key while already transformed now ends Symbiosis directly.
