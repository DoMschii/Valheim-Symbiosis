v3.8.2 - Global F6 perspective persistence

- F6 is now one global Ego/Third choice shared by the human player and every symbiosis form.
- Valheim Ego no longer mistakes its temporary suspended FirstPersonEnabled=false state during symbiosis for a real switch to Third Person.
- Toggling F6 inside any symbiosis writes the same per-player perspective preference used by Valheim Ego.
- Leaving symbiosis restores the human player into the perspective currently selected in the creature, instead of restoring a stale pre-possession value.
- Full game restarts therefore preserve Ego reliably.
- No movement/combat/camera-position tuning changed.
