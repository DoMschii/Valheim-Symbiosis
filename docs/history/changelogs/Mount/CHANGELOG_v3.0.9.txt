Valheim_Workers_Mount v3.0.9 - Drake Dive Momentum Stamina

- Drake Sturzflug (Shift+W) no longer costs stamina; it is treated as gravity-assisted diving.
- A committed dive of at least 0.45s grants 2.0s of free upward flight after leaving the dive.
- After the momentum window expires, upward flight costs 10 stamina/second.
- Level flight and descending flight do not consume movement stamina.
- If stamina runs out while climbing, positive lift is removed but horizontal flight remains controllable.
- v3.0.8 native animation/travel assist, head, camera and all other forms are preserved.
