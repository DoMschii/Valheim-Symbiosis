Valheim Workers Mount v4.0.2 - Troll Native Impact Sync

- Native Troll LMB Character.Damage is no longer allowed to resolve on mouse-down.
- Troll1 fist hits are replayed at ~0.56 s, aligned with visible fist contact.
- Troll2 log hits are replayed at ~0.70 s, aligned with the heavy log swing contact.
- Natural-object fist damage uses the same impact timestamp as the native character hit.
- Bodenschlag/Stamm-Slam and Schockgebruell impact timing was moved later to match their visible contact better.
- Custom delayed Troll abilities bypass the native-hit deferral so they never get delayed twice.
- Pending hits are cleared on body/form transitions to prevent ghost damage.
- No balance, camera, movement or other-form gameplay changes.
