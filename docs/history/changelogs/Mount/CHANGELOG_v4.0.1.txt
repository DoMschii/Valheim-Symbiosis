Valheim Workers Mount v4.0.1 - Troll Attack Impact Timing

- Troll custom damage is no longer applied on the mouse-down frame.
- Unarmed LMB keeps Valheim's native character-hit timing; only the extra tree/natural-object breacher hit is delayed to the fist impact (~0.38s).
- RMB Bodenschlag/Stamm-Slam starts animation immediately but applies enemy/world damage at the visible ground impact (~0.58s).
- MMB Schockgebruell applies its damage/push/stagger at the roar impact (~0.42s), not at click time.
- Stamina and cooldowns are still committed on activation, so input/cooldown behavior remains deterministic.
- Pending Troll impacts are cancelled safely on body/form reset so no delayed ghost hit can land after a Troll1/Troll2 swap or dismount.
- No movement, camera, damage values, radii, star scaling, log durability totals, or other forms were changed.
