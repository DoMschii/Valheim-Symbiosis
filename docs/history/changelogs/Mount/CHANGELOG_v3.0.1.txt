v3.0.1 - Troll unarmed tree damage restore

- Restores natural-object damage for Troll1 LMB while unarmed.
- A successful native unarmed LMB now also applies a short forward breacher hit to natural destructibles only.
- Trees/logs/natural blockers can again be damaged with the bare Troll punch; player-built Pieces and Characters are excluded from this extra world hit.
- Troll2 log attacks, STAMM-SLAM, log durability, cameras and all other forms are unchanged.
