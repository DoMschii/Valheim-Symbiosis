Valheim_Workers_Mount v2.0.1

- Troll log pickup now prefers the TreeLog directly under the camera/crosshair up to 8 m away.
- Nearby fallback radius increased to 6 m so the Troll's large body no longer makes valid logs easy to miss.
- Fallen-log recognition expanded for common Valheim log naming/layout variants and parent/child Rigidbody/Destructible placement.
- Pickup probe interval reduced to 0.10 s for a much more responsive [E] prompt.
- Existing v2.0.0 durability, visible log weapon, world-drop-on-exit and combat behavior preserved.
