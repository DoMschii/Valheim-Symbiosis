Valheim Workers Mount v0.1.13

- Based on v0.1.12.
- Third-person camera behavior is unchanged.
- During active Drake symbiosis vanilla GameCamera.UpdateCamera is skipped via Harmony.
- This gives the Mount runtime exclusive ownership of Camera.main and prevents the vanilla camera from jumping to its own pose before the Mount corrects it.
- Intended fix: remaining first-person flicker / motion-vector jitter while preserving the stable third-person camera.
- Valheim Ego suspension from v0.1.12 remains active.
