v3.0.0 - Troll log heavy slam

- Troll2 RMB is now labelled STAMM-SLAM in the existing form HUD.
- While a log is held, ground slam gains +45% enemy damage, +20% radius, +25% push and +50% damage to natural destructibles.
- A log-powered slam consumes 2 of the existing 12 log durability.
- Troll1/unarmed RMB remains the existing normal Bodenschlag with unchanged balance.
- Log depletion still deterministically swaps Troll2 back to Troll1.
- No changes to Drake, Werewolf, Sea Serpent, cameras or movement.
