Valheim_Workers_Mount v2.0.0

- Troll symbiosis now always starts visually unarmed; native/random log weapon renderers are suppressed on entry.
- While unarmed, a fallen Valheim TreeLog within 4.5 m shows "[E] Baumstamm aufnehmen".
- Pressing E consumes the fallen world log and equips the Troll's native log visual.
- Held log has 12 swings of durability.
- Native Troll LMB hits while holding the log gain x1.35 blunt damage plus +35 chop damage.
- When durability reaches zero the log breaks and the Troll returns to unarmed combat.
- Leaving Troll symbiosis normally while an intact log is held drops the original log prefab back into the world.
- Log lookup uses a small local physics query only while Troll form is active and unarmed; no global or idle world scan was added.
- v1.9.1 Serpent stamina regeneration fix and all previous form systems remain preserved.
